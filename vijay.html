Chandrasekaran Joseph Vijay (born June 22, 1974), popularly known as Thalapathy Vijay, is an Indian politician and former actor currently serving as the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu. He took office on May 10, 2026, after leading his party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), to victory in the 2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election.
